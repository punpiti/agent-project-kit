## Story and Delivery QA

- [ ] Audience, occasion, duration, delivery mode, intended action, and core message are explicit.
- [ ] Storyline and transitions remain coherent in montage/contact-sheet view.
- [ ] Every slide has one job; opening, sections, and close support the objective.
- [ ] Timing includes interaction, media, demonstrations, and questions.
- [ ] Speaker notes add delivery value and do not contradict visible content.

## Visual and Accessibility QA

- [ ] Project dimensions, master/theme, grid, margins, fonts, and language rules are correct.
- [ ] Text, charts, labels, equations, citations, and footnotes are readable at delivery scale.
- [ ] No overflow, clipping, substitution, off-canvas object, low-resolution asset, or unresolved placeholder remains.
- [ ] Contrast, reading order, non-color cues, alt text, captions, and reduced-motion needs are checked as applicable.
- [ ] Figures/tables/charts support the slide job and remain traceable to approved sources/data.

## Behavior and Output QA

- [ ] Links, notes, animation, transitions, triggers, audio/video, captions, and offline behavior work as promised.
- [ ] Every required output opens in its target application/viewer without repair warnings.
- [ ] Editable features and cross-format exports preserve what the project promises.
- [ ] Static exports retain meaning that would otherwise depend on animation.
- [ ] Author-only notes, private data, placeholders, and excluded assets are absent from final outputs.
- [ ] Rendered montage and full-slide inspection were completed after the final source change.
