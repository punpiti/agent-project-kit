# 20 — Administrative & Professional Operations

Use for compliance tracking, deadlines, formal submissions, correspondence,
nomination/application dossiers, meeting follow-up, document control, and
professional case management.

## Route

1. Identify the required outcome, authority/recipient, deadline, source of the
   requirement, and current status.
2. Separate verified facts, inferred status, missing evidence, and actions.
3. Build the smallest actionable checklist ordered by deadline and dependency.
4. Reuse verified document inventories and status records; do not rescan folders
   or re-extract documents unless inputs changed.
5. Check completeness, required format, approvals/signatures, naming, delivery
   channel, confidentiality, and proof of submission.
6. Draft communications with explicit owner, requested action, and response date.
7. Never mark submitted, accepted, approved, or complete without evidence.

Load Educational Policy Development only when rules or institutional decisions
are being designed, rather than merely followed or administered.
