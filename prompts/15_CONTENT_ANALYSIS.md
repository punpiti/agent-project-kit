# 15 — Content Analysis

Use for systematic analysis of documents, interviews, open-ended responses,
media, policies, transcripts, curricula, or other textual/visual corpora.

## Route

1. State the analysis question, corpus, unit of analysis, and intended use.
2. Determine whether the method is qualitative, quantitative, or mixed.
3. Define or validate the coding frame before coding the full corpus.
4. Preserve traceability from finding to source excerpt/item without exceeding
   copyright, privacy, or confidentiality constraints.
5. Separate observed content, coded interpretation, and broader inference.
6. Report exclusions, missing material, ambiguity, and coder/judgment risks.
7. For consequential work, pilot a sample and revise the codebook before the
   full pass; do not rerun corpus ingestion/coding when a valid artifact exists.

Load research prompts only for evidence/context work and data-analytics prompts
only when coded content is being summarized quantitatively.
